Most frameworks are long. Very few of the pages matter on a first read. Here is
the order we go in.

## 1. Establish scope

Before anything else, answer: **does this apply to us, and to which systems?**
Look for the applicability or scope section. Note the triggers — data types,
revenue thresholds, headcount, sector, geography.

## 2. Find the obligations, not the prose

Skip the preamble. Go straight to the operative requirements. Group them:

1. Governance — policies, roles, sign-off.
2. Technical controls — access, encryption, logging, backups.
3. Process — incident response, vendor review, training.
4. Evidence — what you must be able to show, and how often.

## 3. Mandatory or voluntary

A legal requirement and a voluntary certification are different projects with
different deadlines. Confirm which one you are looking at, and who enforces it.

## 4. Write down the gaps

For each obligation, one line: *have it*, *partially have it*, or *missing*.
That list is your plan.
