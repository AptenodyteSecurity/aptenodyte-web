Welcome to the Aptenodyte blog. This is where we will write about the things we
think about every day: compliance frameworks, security practice, and the
decisions that go into building the product.

## What to expect

- **Framework deep dives.** Plain-language walkthroughs of the frameworks in our
  [Resources library](/resources) — scope, obligations, and what actually
  changes for a small team.
- **Build notes.** How we are putting Aptenodyte together, and why.
- **Field notes.** Lessons from real compliance work, with the specifics
  removed.

## How these posts are written

Every post is a Markdown file in `src/content/blog/`. Frontmatter at the top of
the file carries the title, date, author, and tags. Drafts stay out of
production until the `draft` flag is flipped.

> Informational only. Nothing here is legal advice or an audit opinion.
